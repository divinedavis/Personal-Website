Spendcap — Support

Spendcap watches your bank account and tells you the moment a day's spending crosses your cap. If something isn't working or you have a question, you're in the right place.

How do I connect my bank?

Open Settings → Connect a bank. Plaid's secure window opens, you pick your bank and sign in there. Your banking login goes to Plaid, not to us. Most banks connect in under a minute; the first sync pulls in up to two years of history.

Why is today's spending behind?

Banks post transactions on their own schedule. A card swipe usually appears within minutes as pending, but some banks hold weekend purchases until Monday and date them then. Spendcap checks for new transactions every hour and whenever your bank tells Plaid something changed. Pull down on Activity to check right now.

When do I get a notification?

Once when the day's spending reaches 80% of your daily cap ("getting close") and once when it passes 100% ("over cap"). One of each per day, never more. Change the cap from Months or Trends; turn alerts off in iOS Settings → Notifications.

What is the monthly cap and the budget lines?

The daily cap drives the alerts. The budget lines (Food, Rent, Transport and so on) plan a month, and their total becomes the monthly cap that Trends and Months judge the month against. Each line has rules that decide which transactions land in it; tap any transaction to move it and the app remembers that choice for next time.

What does "free to spend this week" mean?

It is the money left in your untagged lines (the ones not marked rent, debt, savings, transport, or personal care) spread across the days left in the month, shown a week at a time. Spend less than the week's share and next Monday opens bigger; spend more and the shortfall comes off the rest of the month.

A transaction is in the wrong category

Tap it, pick the right line, and the app writes a rule so the same merchant lands there from now on. You can see and delete rules under each budget line.

How do I disconnect a bank?

Settings → Linked accounts, swipe the bank, Disconnect. That removes the connection and its transactions from Spendcap. You can also revoke the connection on Plaid's side at my.plaid.com.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. Everything is removed immediately: bank connections, transactions, statements, budgets, and the account itself. If you would rather we do it, email the address below from the address you signed up with.

I forgot my password

From the sign-in screen tap Forgot password and enter your email. If you signed up with Apple or Google there is no password to reset; use that button instead.
