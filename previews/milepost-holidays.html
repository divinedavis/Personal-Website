Milepost holidays

Holiday themes like Marracat's, for Milepost: Marracat's 21 days minus its three shopping days, plus two running days (Global Running Day and NYC Marathon Sunday), so 20 in all. Pick one; each shows its launch screen, the Record screen in its colours, and the App Store event card. Every icon is below.

Every holiday

How it works in the app (same as Marracat): one list (holidays.json) drives everything. A theme runs for the days before each holiday; when two overlap, the shorter one wins. The app icon switches to the holiday icon automatically, on by default, with a switch in Settings to turn it off. iOS shows its own "You have changed the icon" note each time it switches; there is no way around that.

App Store: each holiday becomes an in-app event created about 3 weeks ahead with its card art, submitted automatically one at a time (Apple's rule), plus the holiday header on the App Store page during the theme. Event taps open Milepost on the Record screen.

New for Milepost: Global Running Day (first Wednesday of June) and NYC Marathon Sunday (first Sunday of November). Black Friday, Cyber Monday and the Gift Guide are left out because they are about shopping.
