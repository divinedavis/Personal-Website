Milepost preview

The app today next to two glass looks: clear Liquid Glass, and dark glass like Instagram's tab bar (the same tint as the Marracat preview). The map and routes are a real Apple Maps render of Fort Greene; the Profile screen scrolls so you can see the cards pass under the bar.

What changes with glass: the opaque sheet under Start goes away, so the map (and your orange past routes) fills the whole screen. Sport, Start and History sit on a floating glass panel, the Milepost / 30 days / GPS pills and the locate button turn to glass, and the tab bar becomes a floating capsule with a pill behind the selected tab, the way Marracat's does.

Clear glass vs dark glass: clear glass shows the most map, but street names under the bar fight with the tab labels. Dark glass keeps the labels as readable as today while still letting colour and movement through.

On the phone: this is iOS 26's own Liquid Glass (glassEffect), which bends light at the edges more than a browser can show. Milepost supports iOS 17+, so older phones would get the system's frosted material instead.
