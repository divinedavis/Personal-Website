What's real: sizes, spacing and colours come from the app's code: PillTabBar in RootView.swift (five 72×66 items, 4pt inset), FloatingPill in Components.swift (56pt tall), and Theme.swift. The card photo is from your screenshot. On the iPhone the glass would be iOS's own Liquid Glass, which bends light at the edges a little more than a browser can show.

Dark glass: the same tint and rim as the Marracat bar. The selected tab's label switches from the app's dark teal to a pale teal, because dark teal on charcoal fails Apple's contrast check.
