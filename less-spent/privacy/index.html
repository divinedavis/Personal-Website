Less Spent — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Less Spent ("the app") is an iOS app for cutting down screen time. It has no server of its own: everything it knows about you is kept on your phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Signing in

You sign in with Apple or with Google. The app keeps the result on your phone only:

  • Sign in with Apple — the identifier Apple gives the app, the email address Apple shares (possibly a private relay address), and your name if Apple provides it the first time, used as your display name.
  • Google — the app asks Google for your basic profile and email (the "openid email profile" scopes) and keeps your Google account identifier, name and email address.

These details are stored in the app's storage on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else; Less Spent has no backend and nothing verifies them on a server.

Screen time

The current version shows sample screen-time figures, schedules, streaks and leaderboards generated inside the app; it does not read your real Screen Time data. If a future version uses Apple's Screen Time API, Apple only gives apps opaque tokens for the apps you choose, never app names or your usage history in readable form, and this policy will be updated before that ships.

Service providers

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and app distribution.
  • Google — Google sign-in, if you choose it. Google's privacy policy applies to your Google account.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Your sign-in details stay on your phone until you log out or delete your account. Profile → Delete Account removes them from the phone immediately, as does deleting the app. Because we never receive them, we hold nothing to delete on our side. To remove Less Spent's access from your Apple or Google account, use Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple, or your Google account's third-party connections page.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what personal information Less Spent holds about you, give you a copy, correct it, or delete it. Email divinejdavis@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days and will not treat you differently for asking.

  • California (CCPA/CPRA) — you have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and have not done so in the past 12 months.
  • EEA and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR) — you also have the rights to data portability, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time (for example by turning a permission off in iOS Settings), and to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). We rely on performing the service you asked for (contract) and, for device permissions, your consent. Our service providers are in the United States; data sent to them is transferred there under their standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Children

Less Spent is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top moves and the change is described here.
