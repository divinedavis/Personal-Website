Pulse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Pulse ("the app") is an iOS app for short public posts: text up to 280 characters with an optional photo, replies, likes and follows, alongside automated news accounts. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Your account — if you sign up with email, your email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider). If you use Sign in with Apple, the identifier Apple gives the app and the email address Apple shares, which may be a private relay address. The app does not ask Apple for your name.
  • Your profile — your handle, display name and bio, plus counts of your posts, followers and follows.
  • What you post — the text of each post and reply, and any photo you attach from your camera or photo library. The photo is re-encoded as a JPEG on your phone before upload, which leaves out embedded details such as where it was taken.
  • Your activity — which posts you like and which accounts you follow, with the time.
  • Technical data — our hosting provider records the IP address and time of requests in short-lived server logs for security and debugging.

The app asks for camera and photo library access only when you choose to attach a photo. It does not use your location, contacts or microphone.

Who can see it

Pulse is a public network. Your handle, display name, bio, posts, replies, photos, likes and follows can be seen by anyone who uses the app, including people who are not signed in, and photos are served from a public web address. Your email address and password are never shown to anyone.

Service providers

  • Supabase (United States) — hosts the account system, database and photo storage.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, if you choose it, and app distribution through TestFlight / the App Store.
  • Anthropic — the automated news accounts (such as @nba or @worldnews) can use Anthropic's Claude model to shorten public news headlines taken from publishers' RSS feeds. Only the public headline is sent; none of your data, posts or account details ever goes to Anthropic.
  • News publishers — news posts link to the publisher's article. Opening a link takes you to that publisher's website, where their own privacy policy applies.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or give it to anyone else except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your account, profile, posts, photos, likes and follows are kept until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a short period (days, not months). Private database backups we take to restore the service are kept only as long as needed for that purpose.

Deleting your data

Unfollow or unlike at any time in the app. To delete your account, email divinejdavis@gmail.com from the address on the account (or tell us your handle if you used Sign in with Apple). We delete your account, profile, posts, replies, photos, likes and follows within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what personal information Pulse holds about you, give you a copy, correct it, or delete it. Email divinejdavis@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days and will not treat you differently for asking.

  • California (CCPA/CPRA) — you have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and have not done so in the past 12 months.
  • EEA and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR) — you also have the rights to data portability, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time (for example by turning a permission off in iOS Settings), and to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). We rely on performing the service you asked for (contract) and, for device permissions, your consent. Our service providers are in the United States; data sent to them is transferred there under their standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Children

Pulse is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top moves and the change is described here.
