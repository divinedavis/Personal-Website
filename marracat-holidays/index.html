MARRACAT · HOLIDAYS

Examples for each holiday: splash, app icon, App Store header and In-App Event. Made October 20, 2026.

  • One system: the brand stays type-only on white. A holiday changes the colour of the rule (icon, header, event art), the splash disc, and one line naming the day.
  • Overlaps: a single day wins over a season on its own day. The Holiday Gift Guide wears Thanksgiving's colours Nov 20 – Dec 18; Thanksgiving, Black Friday–Cyber Monday and Christmas (Dec 19–25) take over on their days, then Kwanzaa Dec 26–30, Freedom's Eve Dec 31, New Year Jan 1. Black History Month keeps February except Presidents' Day itself (Feb 15).
  • App icon: the day's colours fill the icon behind a white MARRACAT (no bar). iOS shows its own "You have changed the icon" alert every time an app switches icon, and the App Store listing icon can only change with a new version. Proposed: a "Holiday icons" switch in You › Settings, off by default (no surprise prompts).
  • Header: the App Store header is swapped a few days before each holiday and back after. Each swap goes through Apple review.
  • Events: Apple allows up to 10 published at once and 15 approved per app. Each event is re-created every year automatically, with the dates recomputed.
  • Wording: event copy never says "Black-owned" (standing rule for public copy).
