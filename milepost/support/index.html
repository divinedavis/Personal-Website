Milepost — Support

Milepost records your runs and walks with GPS: time, pace, distance, splits, and the route on a map. Everything stays on your phone. If something isn't working, you're in the right place.

How do I record a run?

Open the app, press Start, and allow location access when iOS asks. The full-screen recorder shows the clock, your current split pace, and distance. Press Pause to stop the clock; from there, Resume to keep going or Finish to save. Give the run a title and tap Save.

Does it keep recording with the screen off?

Yes. Lock the phone or switch apps and the run keeps going until you press Finish. The blue location indicator in the status bar means Milepost is still recording.

Why did it say "No Movement Detected"?

The run covered less than about 10 metres, so there was nothing worth saving. Tap Resume to keep going or Discard to throw it away. This also happens if location access was denied: check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Milepost and choose "While Using the App".

Distance or pace looks wrong

GPS needs open sky. The first few hundred metres after a cold start, or a route under tall buildings and trees, can read short or jumpy. Milepost ignores fixes with poor accuracy and treats very slow movement as standing still, so a run that starts indoors will show distance only once you are outside and moving.

Miles or kilometres?

You → Units. Splits are stored in the unit the run was recorded with, so old runs keep their original splits.

Voice cues

You → Settings → Voice cues: off, every mile, or every half mile (every kilometre / half kilometre in metric). The cue reads your time and last split pace and ducks any music that is playing. Preview voice lets you hear it before a run.

How do I delete a run?

Open the run from Activities, tap the menu in the top corner, then Delete. Deleting the app removes everything.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. There is no account and no server. Runs live in a database on your phone. See the privacy policy.
