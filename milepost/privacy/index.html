Milepost — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Milepost ("the app") is an iOS and Apple Watch app that records your runs, walks and bike rides: time, distance, pace, mile or kilometre splits, and the route you took. Recording needs no account. An optional friends feature lets you share the stats of your activities with people you accept. There are no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

  • Your activities — the start time, duration, distance, pace, splits, the GPS points that make up your route, and the title you give each one. These are saved in a database on your phone.
  • Your settings — miles or kilometres, voice cues, whether you share with friends, and your Apple Health choices.

Routes, GPS points and splits never leave your phone, whether or not you use friends.

Apple Watch

If you record an activity on your Apple Watch, the watch measures your heart rate and the calories you burn as well as your location, and sends the finished activity to your iPhone over Apple’s own encrypted link between your two devices. Nothing recorded on the watch is sent anywhere else. While a workout is running on the watch, your iPhone can show it and pause or end it; that also travels only between your devices.

Apple Health

Milepost can add your activities to Apple Health. This is off until you turn it on in You → Apple Health, and iOS asks your permission separately before anything is written.

  • What is written — each activity as a workout: sport, start and end time, how long you were moving, distance, your splits as laps, and an estimate of the calories you burned. On Apple Watch, the heart rate and calories the watch measured are written too.
  • Your route — a separate switch, and it is off by default. Turn it on and the GPS points of each activity are written to Health as well, so Health can draw your route.
  • What is read — heart rate, calories, steps and running or cycling metrics recorded during your activities, so the app can show them, and your weight, so it can estimate calories more accurately. You can refuse any of these in Health and the app carries on without them.

Apple Health is a database on your own device. Everything Milepost writes to it or reads from it stays on your device. Health data is never sent to our server, never shared with your friends, and never used for advertising or analytics. Deleting an activity in Milepost also deletes the workout Milepost wrote for it.

Location

The app asks for location access when you press Start, and only then. While an activity is being recorded it reads your GPS position, including with the screen off, to measure distance and pace and to draw the route. Recording stops when you press Finish. Location is never read while nothing is being recorded, and it is never sent off the device.

Friends (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Apple to use friends, the following is stored on our server (Supabase, hosted in the United States) and linked to your account:

  • Your account — the identifier Sign in with Apple gives the app, and an email address only if Apple shares one (often a private relay address). The email is not shown to anyone or used to contact you.
  • Your profile — the username you pick and an optional display name. Apple offers your name the first time you sign in; it is only used to suggest a display name and is not stored unless you keep it.
  • Your friends — who you are friends with, requests sent and received, and people you have blocked.
  • Shared activities — for each activity you save while sharing is on: sport, title, start time, distance and moving time. No route, map, GPS point or split.
  • Reports — if you report someone, who you reported and the reason you picked.

Who can see it

People you have accepted as friends see your username, display name and shared activities. Anyone signed in can find you by searching for your username, and sees only your username and display name. Blocking someone ends the friendship and hides you from them. We can see the stored data to run the service and to review reports; it is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any activity from the Block tab; if it was shared, it is deleted from the server too.
  • Turn off Share activities with friends on the You tab to delete every activity you have shared.
  • Delete account on the You tab deletes your account, profile, friendships and shared activities from the server immediately. Activities on your phone stay until you delete them or the app.

Children

Milepost is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
