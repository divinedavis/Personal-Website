Milepost — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Milepost ("the app") is an iOS app that records your runs and walks: time, distance, pace, mile or kilometre splits, and the route you took. It has no account, no server, and no analytics. This policy is short because there is very little to say.

What the app stores

  • Your activities — the start time, duration, distance, pace, splits, the GPS points that make up your route, and the title you give each run. These are saved in a database on your phone and nowhere else.
  • Your settings — miles or kilometres, and whether voice cues are on. Also stored only on your phone.

Location

The app asks for location access when you press Start, and only then. While a run is being recorded it reads your GPS position, including with the screen off, to measure distance and pace and to draw the route. Recording stops when you press Finish. Location is never read while no run is in progress, and it is never sent off the device.

What leaves your phone

Nothing. Milepost makes no network requests of its own. The map you see is drawn by Apple Maps, which is part of iOS and covered by Apple's privacy policy. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporters, and no third-party services.

Data we collect

None. We have no way to see your runs, your location, or anything else about you. If iCloud backup is on for your device, Apple backs the app's data up along with your other app data under your own Apple Account.

Deleting your data

Swipe or use the menu on any activity to delete it. Deleting the app removes every activity and setting with it. There is no account to close.

Children

Milepost is not directed at children under 13 and, since it collects no personal information, cannot collect any from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top moves and the change is described here.
